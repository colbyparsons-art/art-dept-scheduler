Recurring Patterns

1
Art Education track shows chronic underenrollment
Art Education courses appeared below the 12-student minimum in 6 instances across 5 different terms: Elementary Art Education (ART 3223) in Fa 21, Fa 24, and Fa 25; Approaches to Art Education (ART 4133) in Fa 25; Principles of Art Education (ART 3013) and Secondary Art Education (ART 3233) in Fa 26 and Sp 26. This mirrors the declining BFA Art Education enrollment trend visible in the enrollment data and warrants a scheduling or recruitment review for that track specifically.
2
Graduate courses systematically undercounted by the current system flag
The existing SMALL_CLASS flag applies a single threshold (apparently 12) to all sections, missing grad courses that fall between 8 and 11 students. In Fall 2022 alone, four graduate courses — Critique Seminar, Interdisciplinary Ceramics, Art & Decoloniality, and Creative Photography — each enrolled 7 students (one below the grad minimum of 8) without being flagged. Additional grad misses occurred in Fa 21 (History of Art, enr 6) and Fa 26 (Art & Social Movements, enr 6). The corrected standard distinguishes the two tiers.
3
ART 3393 (Prof Practices Visual Arts) is the most frequently underenrolled named course
This required professional development course appeared below the 12-student minimum in three separate terms: Spring 2022 (enr 11), Fall 2022 (enr 10), and Fall 2026 (enr 6). In Fall 2026 the enrollment gap of 6 is the largest single-course shortfall in the dataset for an upper-division course. Because professional practices is a required course for multiple tracks, persistent low enrollment may reflect cohort-size issues rather than scheduling problems.
4
Bracketing addresses viability but does not guarantee it
The department used bracketing in 9 of 11 terms, creating 19 UG/Grad pairs total. Fourteen of those pairs met or exceeded the combined threshold of 12. Five did not — including Fall 2025’s ST: Paint: Public Discourse pair, which had a combined enrollment of just 9, and Spring 2026’s Interdisciplinary Ceramics pair (combined 8, driven almost entirely by the grad side with only 1 undergrad enrolling). When the UG side fails to generate demand, bracketing cannot compensate.
5
Fall 2025 shows an across-the-board enrollment thinning
Seven scheduling concerns appeared in a single fall term — spread across five different course numbers, three area tracks (photography, sculpture, ceramics, art education, graphic design). None of these courses were structurally small; they simply drew fewer students than the previous pattern suggests they should. Given the enrollment context (overall UG headcount held roughly stable), this may reflect scheduling collisions, student distribution across too many elective offerings, or the early stages of the graduation cliff documented in the enrollment analysis.
A note on Fall 2026 data

The Fall 2026 figures in this report are drawn from a snapshot taken September 4, 2026 — approximately two weeks into the semester. Enrollment in active courses typically changes through the add/drop period. Numbers for Fa 26 should be treated as preliminary; some courses currently below threshold may resolve, and some may not.

Structural small sections (not scheduling concerns — click to expand)

The following course types run below the minimum by design and are excluded from the main analysis. They are listed here for completeness.

  • ART 4993 — BFA Exhibition: Enrolls the graduating BFA cohort each term. Ranged 9–11 students across five terms (Fa 21, Fa 22, Sp 23, Fa 23, Fa 25) — always below the UG minimum of 12. This reflects graduating class size, not a scheduling problem.
  • ART 4953 / 5953 — Internship: Multiple sections per term (up to six), each supervising 0–7 individual students in outside placements. By nature individually small. Appeared in every term.
  • ART 5883 / 5963 — MFA Thesis Exhibition: 1–2 students per section, reflecting students at the thesis completion stage. Always below the grad minimum of 8. Multiple sections per term in later years as the MFA cohort expanded.
  • ART 5993 — Thesis: Individual thesis supervision, 1 student per section. Appeared in Sp 23 and Sp 26.
  • ART 5843 / 5943 — Art-Based Research Writing: Treated as individual study throughout, equivalent to Independent Study. The course has always functioned as individualized dissertation-stage writing supervision regardless of whether it appeared under a single section number (early terms) or multiple individual sections. All instances are excluded from the scheduling concerns analysis.
Methodology
  • Source: Visual Arts Course Schedule 2021–2026 (CLEANED), generated 9/4/2026. Contains 11 academic terms, Fa 2021 through Fa 2026.
  • De-duplication: Each section appears multiple times in the source data (once per meeting component). Enrollment was counted once per unique term + section combination.
  • Active sections only: Cancelled and pending sections (SEC_STATUS ≠ A) were excluded.
  • Exempt categories: Independent Study courses and cross-listed courses (cross-listed with another department) were excluded from the analysis. No cross-department enrollment data is available in this report, so cross-listed courses cannot be evaluated fairly on enrollment alone.
  • Thresholds: Undergraduate courses (1000–4000 level): minimum 12 enrolled. Graduate courses (5000 level): minimum 8 enrolled.
  • Bracketed courses: When two sections are bracketed, their individual enrollments are combined. A mixed UG/Grad pair is evaluated against the higher threshold (12). A UG+UG pair is evaluated against 12. A Grad+Grad pair would use 8, though none appeared in this dataset.
  • 499 unique active non-exempt sections were analyzed in total.
