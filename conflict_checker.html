⛔ Hard Conflicts — Scheduling Impossibilities

These are physical impossibilities that must be fixed before anything else. No override or exception applies.

  • Faculty double-booking: Same instructor assigned to two MW or TTh courses in the same time block (unless they are a bracketed pair)
  • Room double-booking: Same room assigned to two MW or TTh courses in the same time block (unless they are a bracketed pair)

🔴 Tier 1 — Must Never Overlap

These courses are required by all or most students with no substitutions. Scheduling any two in the same time slot prevents students from taking both.

  • Art Education sequence: ART 3013 · ART 3223 · ART 3233 · ART 4133
  • Universal capstone cluster: ART 3173 · ART 3393 · ENG 3433 · ART 4993

🟡 Tier 2 — Avoid Within Track/Focus

Students in specific tracks or focus areas commonly need both courses. Overlapping them creates a conflict for that population.

  • GD required: 2243↔2293 · 2293↔3253 · 4363↔4253 · 4363↔4283 · 4363↔4313
  • GD Illustration focus: 3383↔3363 · 3383↔4483
  • Studio 3D focus: 3683↔3793 · 3683↔3893 · 4013↔3793
  • Studio Painting focus: 3703↔3723 · 3703↔4703
  • Studio Photography focus: 3693↔4653 · 4683↔4653
  • BA Art History electives: 4043↔4053 · 4543↔3333
  • Art Education: 3013↔4133 · 3013↔3223 · 3013↔3233

ℹ️ Tier 3 — High-Demand Courses (informational)

These courses appear in many programs and minors. Conflicts cannot be fully avoided but are noted for awareness.

  • ART 3173 Contemporary Issues — required ALL tracks + Art History minor
  • ART 2223 Digital Art Foundations — in 4 minors; needs multiple sections
  • ART 3393 Prof Practices — capstone for ALL tracks
  • ART 3343 WIVA — required BA + Art History minor
  • ART 3913 Scientific Illustration — in Illustration + Sci Illus minors

📌 Special Rules

  • Bracketed course pairs are automatically excluded from conflict warnings
  • ENG 3433 is taught by English dept — must be worked around, not moved by us
  • Art Ed courses: only one offered per semester, but must not share a time slot
  • ART 3173 Contemporary Issues offered every semester — protect its slot carefully
